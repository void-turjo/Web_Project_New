Official Course Study Material & Key Concepts Summary

1. Introduction to Cell Biology

Cells are the fundamental structural and functional units of all living organisms. All living things are composed of one or more cells.

Core Cell Doctrine:
  • Cell Membrane: Semi-permeable lipid bilayer controlling substance transport.
  • Nucleus: Contains genetic material (DNA) and directs cellular activities.
  • Mitochondria: Powerhouse of the cell, generating ATP through cellular respiration.

2. Cellular Respiration & Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy (glucose), while cellular respiration breaks down glucose to release ATP energy.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light Energy ➔ C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (Photosynthesis)
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ ➔ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP Energy (Cellular Respiration)

3. Genetics & DNA Inheritance

DNA double helix structure encodes instructions for protein synthesis and hereditary traits passed down according to Mendel's Laws of Inheritance.
