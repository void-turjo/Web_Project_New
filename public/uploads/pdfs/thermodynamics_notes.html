1. The Four Laws of Thermodynamics

• Zeroth Law: Thermal equilibrium defines temperature (if A=B and B=C, then A=C).
• First Law (Energy Conservation): ΔU = Q - W (Internal Energy = Heat added - Work done).
• Second Law (Entropy): Total entropy of an isolated system always increases (ΔS ≥ 0).
• Third Law: Entropy approaches a constant value as temperature approaches absolute zero (0 K).

2. Heat Transfer & Work Equations

• Heat Capacity: Q = m × c × ΔT
• Latent Heat: Q = m × L
• Work Done by Gas: W = P × ΔV
• Efficiency of Heat Engine (η): η = 1 - (Q_cold / Q_hot) = 1 - (T_cold / T_hot)

3. Enthalpy, Entropy & Free Energy

• Enthalpy: H = U + P·V
• Entropy Change: ΔS = dQ_rev / T
• Gibbs Free Energy: ΔG = ΔH - T·ΔS (Spontaneous if ΔG < 0)
