1. Kinematics Equations (Uniform Acceleration)

• v = u + at
• s = ut + ½at²
• v² = u² + 2as
• s = ½(u + v)t
*(u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time, s = displacement)*

2. Newton's Laws of Motion & Forces

• First Law: F_net = 0 => v = constant (Inertia)
• Second Law: F = m × a (Force = Mass × Acceleration)
• Third Law: F_A->B = - F_B->A (Action & Reaction)
• Weight: W = m × g (g ≈ 9.81 m/s²)

3. Work, Energy & Power

• Work Done: W = F × d × cos(θ)
• Kinetic Energy: KE = ½ m v²
• Potential Energy: PE = m g h
• Power: P = W / t = F × v
